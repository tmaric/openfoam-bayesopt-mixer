Bayesian Optimization in Computational Fluid Dynamics

Two slide decks from the Delft Workshop on Bayesian Optimization for CFD.

Theory

From the Gaussian distribution to Gaussian processes, acquisition functions, multi-objective and multi-fidelity optimization. Self-contained.

Hands-on tutorial

A micromixer optimized end to end with CadQuery, OpenFOAM and BoTorch, six exercises with answers, and how to reproduce the study.

Arrow keys or space to advance, Home / End for the ends, N for the speaker notes.
